Policies & Terms

Payments

Dragon Wraps TX accepts all major forms of payment including cash, card, and approved financing options. Payment must be made in full or according to agreed terms before work is completed.

No Refund Policy

Once materials have been ordered for your vehicle, all payments are non-refundable. This is due to the custom nature of vinyl wraps and specialty materials being purchased specifically for your project.

Customer Commitment

We are committed to working with every customer to ensure satisfaction. If any issues arise, we will do our best to find a fair and reasonable solution. Communication is key and we’re here to help throughout the process.

Vehicle Condition

Customers are responsible for ensuring their vehicle is in suitable condition for wrapping. Existing paint damage, rust, or poor previous repairs may affect final results.

Turnaround Time

Project timelines may vary depending on vehicle condition, wrap complexity, and material availability. We will always communicate realistic timeframes before starting.
