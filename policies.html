Estimates & Approvals

All estimates are based on the information available at the time of inspection. Additional labor, repairs, or materials required after work begins may result in additional charges. Approval of an estimate, invoice, quote, design, or work order constitutes authorization to proceed with the project.

Deposits & Materials

A deposit may be required before scheduling or beginning work. Once vinyl, graphics, specialty materials, or project-specific products have been ordered, all deposits become non-refundable. Materials are purchased specifically for each customer's vehicle and cannot typically be returned or reused.

Payments

Dragon Wraps TX accepts cash, major credit cards, and approved financing options. Final payment is due immediately upon project completion and before vehicle release unless otherwise agreed to in writing. Failure to make payment when due may result in storage fees and other remedies available under applicable law.

Dragon Wraps TX is not responsible for damage resulting from pre-existing vehicle conditions.

Turnaround Time

Project timelines may vary based on vehicle condition, wrap complexity, weather, design revisions, material availability, supplier delays, or unforeseen circumstances. Estimated completion dates are not guarantees.

Completion Notification

Customers will be notified when a project is substantially complete and ready for pickup. Notification may be provided by phone call, text message, email, social media message, or any contact method provided by the customer.

Vehicle Storage Fees

Vehicles not picked up within twenty-four (24) hours after completion notification may incur storage fees of $50.00 per calendar day until the vehicle is collected and all outstanding balances have been paid in full. Storage fees are cumulative and become part of the customer's outstanding balance.

Abandoned Vehicles

Vehicles left on the property without payment, pickup arrangements, or communication for an extended period may be considered abandoned to the extent permitted by Texas law. Dragon Wraps TX reserves all rights and remedies available under applicable law regarding unpaid balances, storage fees, and abandoned property.
